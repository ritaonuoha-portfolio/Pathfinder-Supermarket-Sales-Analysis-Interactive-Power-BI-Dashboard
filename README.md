# Pathfinder Supermarket Sales Analysis & Interactive Power BI Dashboard

## Introduction

The Pathfinder Supermarket Sales Analysis project is a Power BI data analytics project focused on evaluating supermarket sales performance and generating meaningful business insights from transactional data.

The objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear overview of business performance across revenue, orders, units sold, customers, product categories, regions, payment methods, discounts, and monthly sales trends.

As part of the analysis, I prepared and transformed the available data to make it suitable for analysis and visualization. I then developed key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and interactive visualizations in Power BI to present the data in a clear and business-focused format.

The dashboard provides an overview of approximately $5M in revenue, 5K orders, 20K units sold, and 989 customers. It also shows an average customer rating of 3.0 and an average order value of approximately $1.02K.

The analysis shows that Electronics was the highest revenue-generating product category at approximately $1.83M, followed by Clothing at approximately $1.53M. The West region recorded the highest revenue at approximately $1.35M. Electronics also recorded the highest quantity sold, with approximately 7.1K units.

The dashboard further examines payment behavior, with Card being the most frequently used payment method, followed by Cash on Delivery and Wallet. Monthly revenue trends were also analyzed to identify fluctuations in sales performance throughout the year.

The findings provide a high-level view of the supermarket's sales performance and highlight areas that may require further investigation, including customer satisfaction, product performance, regional sales, discount levels, and monthly revenue fluctuations.

This project demonstrates my practical skills in data preparation, data analysis, KPI development, data visualization, and interactive dashboard development using Microsoft Power BI. It also demonstrates my ability to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

The objective of this project was to analyze Pathfinder Supermarket's sales data and answer key business questions, including:

## Key Insights

### Product Category Performance

Electronics generated the highest revenue at approximately $1.83M, followed by Clothing at approximately $1.53M. Home generated approximately $982K, while Beauty generated approximately $766K.

### Regional Performance

The West region recorded the highest revenue at approximately $1.35M, followed by North at approximately $1.28M. South and East generated approximately $1.25M and $1.24M respectively.

### Monthly Revenue Trend

Revenue fluctuated throughout the year, with noticeable increases during certain months, particularly May and August. Further investigation into seasonal demand, promotions, and product availability could help explain these fluctuations.

### Payment Methods

Card payments recorded the highest number of orders with 2,270 transactions, followed by Cash on Delivery with 1,774 and Wallet with 956.

### Units Sold by Category

Electronics recorded the highest quantity sold at approximately 7.1K units, followed by Clothing at approximately 6.2K units. Home recorded approximately 3.9K units, while Beauty recorded approximately 3.0K units.

## Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing categories, particularly Electronics and Clothing.
- Investigate the reasons behind monthly revenue peaks and lower-performing periods.
- Continue supporting card payment options while monitoring the adoption of other payment methods.
- Review discount levels across product categories to understand their effect on sales performance.
- Investigate the average customer rating of 3.0 to identify potential customer experience issues.
- Conduct further product-level analysis to identify high-performing and underperforming products.
